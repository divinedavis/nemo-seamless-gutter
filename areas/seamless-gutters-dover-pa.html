Gutter Repair in Dover, PA

Most repair calls in Dover come down to a handful of problems: a section that has pulled away from the fascia, a seam that has opened up and started dripping, or a downspout that has come loose after a windstorm. Because seamless gutter is formed in one continuous run, a leak usually means a failed end cap, a loose hanger, or a joint at a corner or downspout drop - not a crack in the gutter body itself. We can usually diagnose the cause from the ground and confirm it on the ladder before quoting anything.

Not every problem needs a full replacement. If the aluminum is still straight, holding its pitch, and free of rust-through, we will reseal, re-hang, or re-pitch it and send you photos of what was fixed. Where we run into trouble on some of the older Dover farmhouses and infill homes is fascia board that has softened from years of overflow - in those cases the gutter is fine, but the wood behind it needs attention before a repair will hold. We will tell you straight if that is what is going on rather than just recaulking over it.

Ice damming and heavy fall leaf load are the two things that generate the most repair calls in this area once the weather turns. A gutter that is sagging or spilling over in one particular spot, rather than along its whole length, is almost always a localized fix - loose hangers, a clogged outlet, or a downspout that needs to be re-run - and that is usually a same-visit repair, not a project.

  • Sagging or pulling-away sections from failed or missing hangers
  • Leaking seams, corners, and end caps
  • Downspouts knocked loose or crushed by storms, ladders, or mowing equipment
  • Overflow at one spot from a clog or a low point in the run
  • Fascia board softened behind the gutter that needs to be addressed before or during the repair

Can a seamless gutter actually be repaired, or does the whole run have to be replaced?

In most cases it can be repaired. Seamless gutter fails at its connection points - hangers, end caps, corners, downspout drops - long before the aluminum itself gives out, so a repair usually means resecuring or resealing those points rather than tearing out the run. Full replacement only makes sense when the metal has rusted through, been bent out of pitch in multiple spots, or is undersized for the roof it is draining. Common questions

How much does gutter installation cost in Dover?

Price depends on how many feet of gutter the house needs, whether you go 5-inch or 6-inch, the number of downspouts, roof height and stories, and whether old gutter has to come down first. We measure the house and give you a free written estimate before any work starts.

Should I put 5-inch or 6-inch gutters on my Dover home?

Five-inch K-style handles most Dover houses fine. Six-inch makes sense when you have a large or steep roof dumping a lot of water into one run, long gutter runs, or a history of water pouring over the front edge during heavy storms. We look at the roof before recommending either.

How long does the installation take?

A typical single-family home in Dover is usually a one-day job. We form the gutter on site, hang it, run the downspouts and clean up the same day. Larger homes, steep or multi-story roofs, or fascia repair can push it into a second day.

Can you replace just the section that's leaking?

Sometimes. If one run has pulled loose or a corner is leaking and the rest is sound, a partial replacement works. But if the gutter is sectional with joints every ten feet, or the fascia behind it is soft, replacing the full run is usually the better fix. We'll tell you which you're looking at.

Do you install gutter guards at the same time?

Yes. Dover has plenty of mature oaks and maples, and guards get installed as part of the same job when you want them. We'll talk through what your tree cover actually calls for rather than putting guards on runs that don't need them.
